How It Works

Fill in the story, one clue at a time.

  1. Readers choose the topic

    A new issue comes out every month, each one built around something everyone is talking about: a show, a book series, an artist, a mystery. Readers vote on the topics they want to see next.

  2. The title holds the secret

    Every book is called From the Library of…, and its topic finishes the title. The topic stays hidden until the reveal, with clues dropped along the way. Follow us on Instagram and TikTok to spot them.

  3. The book comes to you

    Order the paperback and it's printed and mailed to your door.

  4. Solve, score, unlock

    Work through the puzzles, mark them on your tracker and collect points toward limited editions and prizes.

Why the raven?

Corvids are the family of birds that includes the raven, and they are famous for solving puzzles and riddles. In old storybooks, the raven is a magical bird. Ours keeps the key to the library.
