The Book

A puzzle book painted like a manuscript from an old storybook, and filled with everything the world is talking about this month.

Inside Every Issue

  • This month's world, in an ancient hand. Every puzzle is about current topics: the shows, songs, trends, memes, celebrities and headlines of the moment. The topics are voted on by readers each month.
  • A mix from the bestiary. Crosswords, ciphers, riddles, logic puzzles, mazes and more. New kinds of puzzle join the books over time, and the ones readers love most stay. See every puzzle.
  • The tracker on the first page. Keep count of how many puzzles you solve each week and month.
  • Points toward treasure. Your solves earn points, and points open the door to limited edition books and prizes.
  • The ravens' final secret. The answers from every chapter combine at the end of the book to unlock one last puzzle.
  • Painted like a storybook. Gold-leaf borders, stained-glass windows, castles, dragons, fairies, flowers and a touch of the circus, with a raven watching from every margin.
