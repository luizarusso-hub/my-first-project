✦ Twenty-Seven Kinds of Puzzle ✦

A Bestiary of Puzzles

Old-world puzzles, every one of them hiding something from this month's feed. Not every puzzle lives in every book: new ones join over time, and the ones readers love most are kept.

Words & Letters

Old manuscripts hiding new words.

I

Crossword

A grid painted onto a manuscript page with gold-leaf borders, where the clues are old proverbs that hide modern answers.

II

Cryptic Crossword

Each clue is a riddling line of verse from a scribe, with wordplay leading to answers like STREAMING or PLAYLIST.

III

Word Search

A dense block of blackletter script that looks like an ordinary page of ancient text, secretly hiding trending words.

IV

Anagram

A royal proclamation whose scrambled words, once rearranged, reveal a famous modern phrase.

V

Word Ladder

A vine climbing up the margin, with one letter changing on each leaf until one word transforms into another.

VI

Acrostic

A poem in an illuminated border, where the decorated first letter of each line spells a secret message.

VII

Rebus

A row of tiny medieval pictures (a raven, a key, a crown) that combine to spell out a modern word or phrase.

Secret Codes

Messages the ravens have locked away.

VIII

Substitution Cipher

A message written in an invented illuminated alphabet, with a partial key hidden elsewhere in the book.

IX

Caesar Cipher

A rotating wheel drawn like an astrolabe, which you turn to shift the letters and decode a prophecy.

X

Pigpen Cipher

Mysterious symbols carved on a tomb or a tower door, decoded with a grid drawn on another page.

XI

Hidden Message

A normal-looking chronicle where every tenth word, or every gold-inked letter, spells out a secret.

Logic & Numbers

For those who like their answers proven.

XII

Grid Logic Puzzle

A tournament scene where you work out which knight, crest and horse belong together, the knights being rival fandoms.

XIII

Rune Sudoku

A 9×9 grid set inside a stained-glass window, using runes or heraldic symbols instead of numbers.

XIV

Nonogram

A shading grid that, once solved, reveals a hidden picture, such as a raven holding a modern object.

XV

Truth-teller & Liar

Three castle guards each make a statement. Work out who lies to find the right door.

XVI

Chess Problem

A painted chessboard with a few pieces placed, asking you to find the move that wins the game.

XVII

Magic Square

A grid of Roman numerals engraved on a wizard's seal, where every row, column and diagonal adds up to the same number.

XVIII

Number Sequence

A row of coins, candles or bells. Work out the pattern to find the next one.

XIX

Measurement Puzzle

An alchemist must measure an exact amount of potion using only two odd-sized flasks.

Riddles & Tales

Solemn old words about very modern things.

XX

“What Am I?” Riddle

A short verse in solemn old English describing something modern. Try the one below.

XXI

Lateral-Thinking Story

A strange tale from the kingdom (“the queen wept when the glowing mirror went dark”) that you must explain.

Pictures & Hunts

Look closely. Something doesn't belong.

XXII

Maze

A labyrinth drawn as castle corridors, forest paths or the winding roads of the quest map.

XXIII

Spot the Difference

Two nearly identical manuscript illustrations where a few details differ, some of them sneaky modern items.

XXIV

Hidden Object

A crowded painted scene of a medieval market where you hunt for modern things disguised in the crowd.

XXV

Tangram & Mosaic

Painted tile shapes, like those on an antique puzzle box, that rearrange into a silhouette.

The Grand Quest

Where every answer leads somewhere.

XXVI

Quest Map

A painted fantasy map where each landmark holds a puzzle, and the answers reveal the path to the treasure.

XXVII

Meta Puzzle

The answers from every chapter combine at the end of the book to unlock the ravens' final secret.

Tell the Ravens

Which puzzles do you love most? Which ones should stay, and which should the ravens retire? The books change with their readers, so every choice counts.
