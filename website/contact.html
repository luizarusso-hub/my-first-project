Questions Oft Asked

What is CorvidzzPuzzles?

A puzzle book company. Our books look like medieval manuscripts, but every riddle, cipher and crossword inside is about today's pop culture: the trends, shows, music, memes and headlines everyone is talking about.

How do I buy a book?

Add Volume I to your basket and press Checkout. Payment is handled securely by Shopify, and shipping options and costs are shown before you pay.

What kinds of puzzles are inside?

Crosswords, ciphers, riddles, logic puzzles, mazes, hidden-object scenes, quest maps and more. New kinds join over time and readers' favourites stay. See the full bestiary.

How is each month's topic chosen?

Readers who have bought a book vote in a monthly poll on the topics they'd like to see next. The chosen topic stays a surprise until the first of the month, with clues along the way. More about the monthly council.

What are points for?

The first page of every book has a tracker for the puzzles you solve each week and month. Solving earns points, and points give you access to limited edition books and prizes.

I have a question about my order.

Write to corvidzzpuzzles@gmail.com with your order number and the ravens will help.

Can I suggest a topic or a puzzle?

Please do. Send it to corvidzzpuzzles@gmail.com or message us on Instagram or TikTok.
