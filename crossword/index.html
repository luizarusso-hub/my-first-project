For your Canva book

1. Download the grid pictures. They're print quality (300 DPI) with a see-through background.

2. Copy the clues, then paste each list into its own Canva text box, so you can style them with your book's fonts.

How to put it on a Canva page
  1. In Canva, open your book and click Uploads → Upload files, and choose the PNG.
  2. Drag it onto the page and resize it from a corner (it stays sharp).
  3. Add two text boxes (Text → Add a little bit of body text) and paste the Across and Down clues into them.
  4. Put the answer key PNG on your answers page at the back of the book.
  5. Changed the clues or layout? Download again and use Replace on the picture in Canva so its size and position stay.
