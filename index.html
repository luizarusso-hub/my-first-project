Once Upon a Time

AAmong all the birds of the old storybooks, none is more magical than the raven. Ravens belong to the family of Corvids, birds famed the world over for solving puzzles and riddles. Our books are named in their honour.

Every page is painted like a manuscript from a fairy tale, full of gold-leaf borders, castles, dragons, knights and flowers. The clues are old proverbs and riddling verse, but the answers are modern, and the topics are the ones the world is talking about right now.

What am I?

Unseen I dwell in every hall,
No body have I, yet answer all.
Sever my bond and the whole court moans;
Speak thou my secret word, and I am thine own.

Reveal the answer
