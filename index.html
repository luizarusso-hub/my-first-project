What lies within

Not all books are alike. Over time new kinds of puzzles join the collection, and the ones readers love most stay.

Voted by the Court

Each month, readers vote on the topics they want to see in the next issues.

A Secret Each Moon

The topic is a surprise, revealed on the first of each month, with clues hidden throughout the month before.

The Puzzle Tracker

The first page of every book keeps count of the puzzles you solve each week and month.

Points & Treasure

Collect points as you solve, and trade them for limited edition books and prizes.

A Bestiary of Puzzles

A glimpse at some of the puzzles being painted for the books.

Crossword

A grid painted onto a manuscript page with gold-leaf borders, its clues written as old proverbs that hide modern answers.

Cryptic Crossword

Each clue a riddling line of verse from a scribe, with wordplay leading to answers like STREAMING or PLAYLIST.

Word Search

A dense block of blackletter script that looks like an ordinary page of ancient text, secretly hiding trending words.

Rebus

A row of tiny medieval pictures (a raven, a key, a crown) that combine to spell out a modern word or phrase.

Rune Sudoku

A 9×9 grid set inside a stained-glass window, using runes or heraldic symbols instead of numbers.

Truth-teller & Liar

Three castle guards each make a statement, and you must work out who lies to find the right door.

Caesar Cipher

A rotating wheel drawn like an astrolabe, turned to shift the letters and decode a prophecy.

Quest Map

A painted fantasy map where each landmark holds a puzzle, and the answers reveal the path to the treasure.

The Meta Puzzle

The answers from every chapter combine at the end of the book to unlock the ravens' final secret.
